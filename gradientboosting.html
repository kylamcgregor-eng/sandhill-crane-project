Overview

Gradient boosting also combines many weak learners in sequence, but each new learner is fit to the residual errors left by the combined model so far rather than to reweighted mistakes. It will be compared against AdaBoost on the same classification task to show how the two boosting strategies differ in accuracy and in which features they lean on most.
